## ffh

A CLI that gathers SSH destinations from configuration and external providers, then connects through fuzzy search.

Example usage:

```console
$ ffh
? Select a host:
> production-web
  staging-api
```

[GitHub: ffh](https://github.com/csnagu/ffh)
